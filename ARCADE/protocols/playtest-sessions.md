# Protocol: Playtest Sessions

Human playtesting is required for fun, feel, clarity, pacing, and taste.

## Session file location

`/docs/validation/playtest-sessions/YYYY-MM-DD-slice-or-feature.md`

## Template

```markdown
# Playtest Session

## Build

## Slice / Feature

## Tester Profile

## Session Duration

## What the tester did

## Where they got confused

## What they enjoyed

## What they ignored

## What broke

## Direct quotes

## Issues to convert into tickets

## Design questions raised

## Recommendation
```

## Rule

Do not treat one player's opinion as universal. Convert repeated or high-impact findings into tickets or design questions.
